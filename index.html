How to use this

Tap + for one standard portion. To log an exact amount, tap + once, then type the grams or millilitres in the Amount box. The nutrients scale with it.

Avoid eating calcium and iron together if possible: calcium reduces iron absorption.

Eat iron together with vitamin C: tomatoes, orange, kiwi, vegetables.

Nutrient values are typical reference figures per 100 g or 100 ml (USDA and CREA tables, some from product labels). Brands and recipes vary, and items marked approximate are estimates, so treat totals as estimates.
